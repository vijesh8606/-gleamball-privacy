Gleamball Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Who we are

Gleamball is a single-player offline game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

Information we collect

None. Gleamball does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Data saved on your device

The game saves your progress (unlocked levels, stars, achievements, play statistics) and your settings (sound, controls, graphics, ball finish) in files inside the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your phone. It is deleted when you use Settings > Reset Progress or uninstall the app.

Internet access

Gleamball does not need or use an internet connection.

Device sensors and permissions

  • Motion sensors (accelerometer / gravity): read only while you play, to steer the ball by tilting. The readings are used instantly and are never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Vibration: a short pulse when the ball hits a wall. You can turn it off in Settings.

Gleamball does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files.

Children's privacy

Gleamball is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and in the app, with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the developer contact email shown on Gleamball's Google Play page.
